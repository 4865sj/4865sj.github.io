About

I am an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student at KAIST working on quantum information theory.

My research focuses on the classical simulation, learning, and computational complexity of quantum systems, particularly fermionic quantum systems.

Research interests

  • Classical simulation of quantum systems
  • Fermionic quantum computation
  • Quantum learning and verification
  • Sampling complexity and anticoncentration
  • Noisy quantum computation
